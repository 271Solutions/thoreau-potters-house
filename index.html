Welcome to The Potter's House

Jesus changes everything.

We are a Bible-believing Christian church committed to preaching the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening families, and reaching people with the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Whether you have followed Jesus for years, are returning to church, or are searching for answers, you are welcome to come hear the Word of God and experience genuine Christian fellowship.
